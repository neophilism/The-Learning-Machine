# Complete Music Learning Lesson

TLM-047 integrates the original-melody synthesis, waveform analysis, musical classifier and expression-comparison engines into one browser-local prediction, experiment, observation, revision and transfer journey. The learner chooses one of two unseen original note sequences and commits a rising/falling prediction before any model result is calculated or displayed.

The experiment trains the existing deterministic softmax learner on four explicitly named training IDs and evaluates four disjoint held-out IDs. It then classifies the selected unseen melody and separately reports exact replay, transformed interval pattern or new-sequence evidence. A model confidence is a model score, not a probability of musical quality, authorship or legal originality. All sequences are authored fixtures synthesized locally; no recording or microphone is required.

The unseen candidate can be played only after an explicit button gesture through the browser's native audio context. Listening is optional: the visible note events, tempo, model result, held-out details and comparison disclosure preserve the complete non-audio route. Browser acceptance covers the gesture-triggered audio preview separately from an explicit keyboard-only route that completes while audio construction is disabled.

After observing the result, the learner must record a bounded revision or proposed next test before running transfer on the other unseen melody. Transfer cannot reuse the first candidate. Completion explicitly records reasoning rather than agreement with the model. Tests cover stage order, split evidence, disagreement, revision bounds, distinct transfer, reset, candidate audio and keyboard/non-audio completion.
