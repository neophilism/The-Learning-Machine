# TLM-060: Independent legal caveats

\`evaluateIndependentLegalCaveats\` demonstrates that a copyright authorization or exception does not answer independent access-control, privacy, contract, or confidentiality questions.

The exercise treats \`17 U.S.C. § 1201\`, interception under \`18 U.S.C. § 2511\`, and federal trade-secret civil-remedy and definition provisions in \`18 U.S.C. §§ 1836 and 1839\` as separate issue-spotting tracks. It never turns a copyright result into permission to circumvent an access control, intercept a communication, disregard a contract, or acquire, use, or disclose confidential information.

Each track fails closed when a material fixture fact is unknown. A “review required” result is not a legal conclusion: consent, authorization, statutory exceptions, reasonable secrecy measures, improper means, contractual duties, defenses, remedies, and applicable state law remain outside the bounded fixture. The authority pack is a learning reference with a stated source baseline, not certified current-law or legal advice.
