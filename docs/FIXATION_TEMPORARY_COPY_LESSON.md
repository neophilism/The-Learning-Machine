# TLM-058: Fixation and temporary-copy lesson

`evaluateFixationLesson` teaches the fixation distinction through bounded, source-linked case records. It presents the statutory “more than transitory duration” requirement in 17 U.S.C. §101, the automatically overwritten 1.2-second buffer record in `Cablevision`, and the maintenance loading of software into usable RAM in `MAI Systems v. Peak`.

The evaluator does not compare an arbitrary duration to 1.2 seconds. The Cablevision outcome is available only when the complete cited fixture is reproduced; a shorter, longer or materially changed buffer record remains unresolved. The MAI outcome is likewise limited to its maintenance-RAM fixture. Other RAM, cache and buffer facts remain unresolved for case-specific analysis.

Every result includes all three authorities and their scope limitations. Fixation alone does not establish infringement, authorization, ownership, an exception or fair use. The lesson is dated educational material, not individualized legal advice or a certified current-law service.
