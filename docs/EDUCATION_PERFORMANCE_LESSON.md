# TLM-059: Education Performance and Fair-Use Lesson

This lesson compares historical and live instruction scenarios without treating an educational label as permission.

## Present-law boundaries

- **17 U.S.C. § 110(1)** applies to performance or display by instructors or pupils during face-to-face teaching activities of a nonprofit educational institution in a classroom or similar instructional place. A knowingly unlawful audiovisual copy defeats that stated exception.
- A private tutor can participate in qualifying institutional instruction, while a school label alone cannot establish the statutory facts.
- A for-profit school is outside § 110(1)'s nonprofit-institution condition. That does not automatically make the use infringing.
- **17 U.S.C. § 107** remains a separate, fact-specific four-factor inquiry. Commercial or nonprofit educational character informs factor one but does not decide fair use.
- Whether a performance is public depends on the audience and transmission facts under § 101; the evaluator reports only bounded audience classifications.

## Proposed educational expansion

The versioned proposal record is currently an incomplete placeholder with no authoritative provision. The lesson therefore reports the expansion as unavailable and never treats it as enacted law or present permission.
